Privacy & data

Privacy Policy

Effective date: 21 October 2026 · NutriTrack V2

NutriTrack V2 is a personal nutrition and wellness tracking web app. This policy explains the information the app uses, how it is stored, and how you can contact us about your data.

1. Information we process

  • Sign-in information: When you choose Google sign-in, our authentication provider may receive basic account information such as your Google account identifier, name, and email address for authentication.
  • Information you enter: Your food diary, calories, protein, fiber, exercise, bowel records, body measurements, goals, saved foods, preferences, and other entries you choose to record.
  • Technical information: Hosting, authentication, and infrastructure providers may process technical logs and information needed to operate and secure the service, such as IP address and browser details, under their own policies.

2. How information is used

We use this information to let you sign in, save and display your records, synchronize them across your devices, and operate and protect the app. NutriTrack V2 is not intended to sell your personal wellness records or use them for advertising.

3. Storage and service providers

NutriTrack V2 stores app records in your browser's local storage and uses Supabase for account authentication and cloud record storage/synchronization. Google is used when you choose Google sign-in, and Vercel hosts the website. These services may process data in different countries according to their infrastructure and policies.

Access to cloud records is designed to be restricted to the signed-in user through database access controls. However, no online service can promise absolute security, and we encourage you to keep your Google account secure.

4. Sharing

Your personal diary and measurements are not intended to be shared with other NutriTrack V2 users. We do not intentionally publish your individual records. Information may be processed by the service providers named above to provide the app, or disclosed where legally required.

5. Retention and deletion

Cloud records are kept while your account remains in use, unless they are deleted or the service is discontinued. Removing the app from your device or clearing your browser does not automatically delete your cloud records. You may remove individual entries using available app features. To request deletion of cloud records associated with your account, contact the email below from the Google account used to sign in (or provide a way to verify ownership). We will review and handle your request, subject to applicable obligations and technical limitations.

6. Your choices

You can choose what information to enter, access or edit records through the app, sign out, and request assistance with your account data. You may also clear local app data using your browser or device settings; this does not necessarily remove cloud copies.

7. Children

NutriTrack V2 is intended for general personal wellness tracking and is not specifically designed for children. If you believe a child has submitted personal information without appropriate permission, please contact us.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app or its data practices change. The updated version and effective date will be shown on this page.

9. Contact

For privacy questions, access requests, or data deletion requests: wanchentian2003.wct@gmail.com.

This app is a personal tracking tool and does not replace professional medical or nutritional advice.

隐私政策

生效日期：2026 年 10 月 21 日

NutriTrack V2 是一款个人营养及健康习惯记录应用。本政策说明我们如何处理你在使用应用时提供的信息。

1. 收集及处理的数据

使用 Google 登录时，身份验证服务可能处理你的 Google 账号标识、姓名及邮箱。应用还会处理你自行输入的饮食、热量、蛋白质、膳食纤维、运动、排便、体重及身体围度、目标与偏好设置。网站托管及身份验证服务可能按照各自政策处理必要的技术日志。

2. 数据用途

相关信息用于账号登录、显示及保存记录、跨设备同步、维持应用运行和安全。我们不打算出售你的个人健康记录，也不打算将这些记录用于广告。

3. 数据存储

记录会保存在浏览器本地存储，并通过 Supabase 进行云端保存与同步。Google 用于登录，Vercel 提供网站托管。这些服务可能在不同国家或地区处理数据。云端数据的访问设计为仅允许已登录用户访问其本人记录，但任何在线服务都无法保证绝对安全。

4. 数据分享

你的个人记录不会主动公开给其他 NutriTrack V2 用户。上述服务提供商可能为了提供服务而处理信息；法律要求时也可能需要披露信息。

5. 保留、删除及你的选择

你可以通过应用已有功能编辑或删除个别记录，也可以选择不填写某些资料。卸载 App 或清除浏览器数据不会自动删除云端记录。如需申请删除账号相关云端数据，请通过下方邮箱联系我们，并提供适当的账号所有权验证。我们会根据适用要求及技术条件处理申请。

6. 儿童使用

本应用并非专门为儿童设计。如你认为儿童在未经适当许可的情况下提供了个人信息，请联系我们。

7. 政策更新

当功能或数据处理方式改变时，本隐私政策可能更新。更新后的内容及生效日期会显示于此页面。

8. 联系方式
